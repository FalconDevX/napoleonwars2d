Command the Grande Armée - one soldier at a time

Wings of Empire is a top-down real-time tactics game set in the Napoleonic Wars, 1805-1815. You lead regiments of infantry, cavalry and artillery across randomly generated battlefields - and every single soldier is simulated: he marches, reloads, fires, fights hand to hand, breaks and runs.

Form a line and trade volleys at two hundred paces. Throw your infantry into square as enemy horsemen wheel in. Bring up the guns, unlimber behind your own lines and send round shot bouncing through their ranks - then switch to canister when they come close. Hit a wavering regiment in the flank with your cuirassiers and watch it come apart.

Features

  • Three arms, each with its own drill - infantry in line or square, volleys on command or fire at will; cavalry in line or wedge; artillery with limbers and horse teams, firing round shot, shell or canister.
  • Morale and fatigue - regiments waver under fire, break when hit in the flank or rear, rout, rally - or surrender when surrounded.
  • Dozens of historical units - full rosters for France and Britain (from fusiliers and the Old Guard to the 95th Rifles, Highlanders and the Scots Greys), each with its own portrait and uniform. Austria, Russia and Prussia are available as additional sides.
  • 9 scenarios - from a skirmish between two brigades to a clash of full corps, a last stand at three-to-one odds, and Conquest, with towns to capture, gold to earn and recruits to raise.
  • Random battlefields - 12 map types (open plain, river crossing, mountain pass, great forest, a ridge on one flank and more) in several theatres of war and seasons.
  • A living battlefield - smoke drifts with the wind, cannon balls plough the earth and leave craters, trees fall, and the field fills with the fallen.
  • Tutorial - Général Duval teaches you to command through hands-on lessons.
  • Multiplayer - host a battle from inside the game, or run a dedicated server.
  • Original soundtrack for the battles, the victories and the defeats.

Controls

  • Left mouse - select a regiment, drag to select several
  • Right mouse - move; on an enemy: fire or charge. Drag to set facing and frontage
  • Mouse wheel - zoom  ·  WASD / arrows - move the camera
  • Q square  ·  V wedge  ·  H halt  ·  T fire at will / on orders  ·  B battery ammunition
  • P pause  ·  + / - game speed up to ×8 (single player)  ·  Esc menu

Requirements

Windows. Built with Godot 4.
